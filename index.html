Runion Neo

Latin with rune-vibes, made on a 3 × 7 grid. Straight lines only, from any dot to any dot. Monospace, narrow and tall, every letter fills the full width of a cell. One pen per weight, from Light to Bold. Unicase: each capital is its lowercase letter ornamented with dots and/or lines. Accents sit outside the letter, two rows above and two below.

The grid — click dots to draw

Pick any glyph below to load it, or draw your own: click a dot to start a line, keep clicking to continue it. Click the last dot again to end the stroke (that also makes a lone dot).

A point is written xy — column, then row. 06 is top-left, 20 bottom-right. Lines join any two points with a dash. That string is the entire definition of a glyph. copy gives the whole line for glyphs.txt: name, character, strokes.
