Runion Neo is Latin with rune-vibes, made on a grid of 3 by 7 dots. Every glyph is made of straight lines from dot to dot, and every letter fills the full width of its narrow, tall, monospaced cell.

The design is sharp and unicase: each capital is its lowercase letter ornamented with dots and/or lines. Weights run from Light to Bold; the dots stay where they are and only the pen changes. Runion Neo is made for headlines, titles, signs and short text.

Its angular look comes from runes, and the font also includes the Elder Futhark at its own codepoints. The typeface is defined in one text file of dot-to-dot connections. To contribute, see github.com/OneManMobile/runion-neo.
