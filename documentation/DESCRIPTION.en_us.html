Runion Neo is a monospaced typeface for Latin and the Elder Futhark, the oldest runic alphabet, drawn on a grid of 3 by 7 dots. Every glyph is made of straight lines from dot to dot, and every letter fills the full width of its narrow, tall cell.

The Latin letters are built the way runes are cut: staves, branches and dots. The design is unicase. Each capital is its lowercase letter with a rune accent: A takes the second arm of ansuz, M the cross of mannaz, H becomes dagaz, E splits its middle line in two, and letters with no line to add take a dot, the way medieval carvers derived new letters from runes. The 24 runes of the Elder Futhark and a few later runes are included at their own codepoints in the Unicode Runic block. Weights run from Light to Bold: the dots stay where they are and only the pen changes.

The typeface is defined in one text file of dot-to-dot connections. To contribute, see github.com/OneManMobile/runion-neo.
