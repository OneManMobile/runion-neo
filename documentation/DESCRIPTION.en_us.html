Runion Neo is Latin with rune-vibes, made on a grid of 3 by 7 dots. Every glyph is made of straight lines from dot to dot, and every letter fills the full width of its narrow, tall, monospaced cell.

The letters are built the way runes are cut: staves, branches and dots. The design is unicase. Each capital is its lowercase letter ornamented with dots and/or lines, many of them taken from runes: A takes the second arm of ansuz, M the cross of mannaz, H becomes dagaz, and letters with no line to add take a dot, the way medieval carvers made new letters from runes. Weights run from Light to Bold: the dots stay where they are and only the pen changes. The 24 runes of the Elder Futhark and a few later runes are included at their own codepoints in the Unicode Runic block.

The typeface is defined in one text file of dot-to-dot connections. To contribute, see github.com/OneManMobile/runion-neo.
